## Collapsible sidebar

Mark a panel `collapsible` to let it snap shut when it is dragged past half its `minSize`, or when Enter is pressed on
the handle. `collapsedSize` keeps room for an icon rail. `onLayoutChange` reports every panel's size in percent, ready to
store and pass back as `defaultLayout`.

## Usage notes

`ResizablePanel` and `ResizableHandle` must be direct children of `ResizablePanelGroup`. Sizes are percentages of the
group; panels without a `defaultSize` share whatever the others leave. Each handle is a focusable `separator` with
`aria-valuenow`, `aria-valuemin` and `aria-valuemax`: the arrow keys move it by `keyboardStep` percent (mirrored in
right-to-left layouts), Home and End jump to the limits, and Enter collapses or restores a collapsible neighbour.
